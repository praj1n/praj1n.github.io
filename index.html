About us

Making home electronics affordable.

SK Terabytes is an OEM and super stockist of LED Smart TVs, based in Chennai. We started our television business in 2024 with one mission: to make home electronics affordable for every home — good screens, smart features and honest service, without the premium price tag.

“Your Trust, Our Promise” is printed on every card we hand out. Everything on this page — the range, the warranty, the terms — is how we keep it.

Our mission

Make home electronics affordable. A good TV shouldn’t be a luxury purchase, so we keep the range simple, the terms clear and the prices honest — and stand behind every set with doorstep service.

Why Xudesi™

The smart choice. We started Xudesi to put a smart, good-looking LED TV within reach of everyday families — Android and WebOS smart platforms, sizes from 24″ to 55″, in a black-and-gold finish that looks the part in any home.

Why Adsan

Top-notch quality for the best price. Adsan is for buyers and dealers who want a dependable TV across the full size range — all the way up to 65″ — at the best price we can offer.

The founder’s journey

A humble start. A bold promise.

The story behind SK Terabytes begins long before the first TV — in Chennai, in 1977.

1977

Sivakumar Kumaraswamy

K. Sivakumar · Proprietor

  1. 1977

    Born in Chennai

    Born into a humble family in Chennai, Tamil Nadu — the city SK Terabytes still calls home.

  2. 2003

    The first engineer in the family

    Graduated with a B.Tech in Electrical & Electronics Engineering — the first engineering graduate in the family.

  3. 2006

    Across the world

    Moved to the USA and worked with major US tech firms, gaining global experience in electronics and IT.

  4. 2016

    Home again

    Returned to India with a desire to innovate locally and contribute to the country’s tech ecosystem.

  5. 2024

    SK Terabytes begins

    Started the television business with a mission to make home electronics affordable — and a promise: “Your Trust, Our Promise.”
