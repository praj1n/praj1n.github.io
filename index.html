The Founder

A humble start. A bold promise.

The story behind SK Terabytes begins long before the first TV — in Chennai, in 1977.

1977

Sivakumar Kumaraswamy

K. Sivakumar · Proprietor

  1. 1977

    Born in Chennai

    Born into a humble family in Chennai, Tamil Nadu — the city SK Terabytes still calls home.

  2. 2003

    The first engineer in the family

    Graduated with a B.Tech in Electrical & Electronics Engineering — the first engineering graduate in the family.

  3. 2006

    Across the world

    Moved to the USA and worked with major US tech firms, gaining global experience in electronics and IT.

  4. 2016

    Home again

    Returned to India with a desire to innovate locally and contribute to the country’s tech ecosystem.

  5. 2025

    The promise takes shape

    The dream of an Indian premium TV brand that rivals the global companies became SK Terabytes — and “Your Trust, Our Promise” became its word.

Our vision

“To make cutting-edge television technology accessible without compromise.”
