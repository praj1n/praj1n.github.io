Privacy

Your details, your call.

This site is a showcase of SK Terabytes and its brands, Xudesi™ and Adsan.

What we collect

This website does not run user accounts and does not store the details you type into the enquiry form. When you press Send on WhatsApp or Send by email, the details you entered (name, phone, company, city, brand, model, quantity, message) are placed into a WhatsApp message or an email on your own device, and only leave your device when you choose to send them.

How we use it

Information you send us is used only to reply to your enquiry — for example, to share the dealer price list or arrange service.

Third parties

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the location map from Google Maps, which may receive standard request data such as your IP address.
  • WhatsApp and your email provider handle messages according to their own policies once you send them.
